Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This OAuth app is used only by its owner, through the open-source tool rclone, to access the owner's own Google Drive.

Data accessed

The app accesses Google Drive files only to upload, download, list and delete the owner's own backup files, at the owner's request.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or sent anywhere other than Google Drive. No analytics or tracking are used.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
